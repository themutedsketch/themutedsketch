# 👋 About Me

I'm an **Electronics & Communication Engineer, Robotics Trainer, and Director of Core Systems at Spaceborn**, working across **embedded systems, robotics, autonomous systems, and control logic**.

At **Spaceborn**, I work in the **FSM (Finite State Machine) department**, focusing on system states, transitions, control logic, and autonomous-system behaviour. I also contribute to the development and coordination of **core system architecture and engineering projects**.

Alongside my engineering work, I work as a **Robotics Trainer**, designing hands-on projects involving Arduino, electronics, sensors, actuators, programming, automation, and robotics.
